About the Course

Fundamentals of Computing introduces the core ideas behind modern computing. Students learn how hardware and software work together, how data is represented and processed, how operating systems and networks function, and how to think through problems step by step the way programmers do.

These foundations apply to every area of computing. Whether the goal is software development, networking, cybersecurity, data work or IT support, the concepts from this course explain what is happening beneath the tools and give students the vocabulary and problem-solving habits to keep learning.

What We Learn

Computer hardware and software, number systems and data representation, operating systems, networks and the internet, and the basics of algorithmic thinking.

How It Applies

These ideas support programming, troubleshooting, system design and responsible technology use, and prepare us for upper-level computing courses.

Skills We Practiced

Research, teamwork, video production, technical writing, presenting and publishing a website with version control.

Our Mission

This website is our team's final project for CMPS1134. Its purpose is to showcase what we learned, introduce the people behind the work, share the videos each of us created, and collect our written report, slides and supporting documentation in one place.

Our approach was to divide the work by strengths, review each other's contributions, and use GitHub to track changes and publish the finished site. We aimed to build something clear, accessible and professional that demonstrates both the course content and the way we collaborated.
